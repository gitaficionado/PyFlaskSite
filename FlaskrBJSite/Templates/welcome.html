How to Play

  1. Log in or create a new account to track your bankroll.
  2. Enter the amount of chips you want to wager.
  3. Select Hit if you want another card drawn.
  4. Select Stay if you are happy with your hand.
  5. The dealer continues receiving cards until reaching at least 17.
  6. The hand closest to 21 without going over wins the pot.

Card Values

  • 2 through 10 are worth face value.
  • Jacks, Queens, Kings are worth 10 points.
  • Aces can be worth 1 or 11 points seamlessly.

Objective of the Game

The objective of Blackjack is to have a hand with a value closer to 21 than the dealer's hand. If your score goes over 21, you bust and lose the hand.
