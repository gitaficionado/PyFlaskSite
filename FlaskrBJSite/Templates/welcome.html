Blackjack

Try to beat the dealer by getting as close to 21 as possible without going over.

Objective of the Game

The objective of Blackjack is to have a hand with a value closer to 21 than the dealer's hand. If your score goes over 21, you lose the hand.

How to Play

  1. Log in or create a new account.
  2. Enter the amount you want to bet.
  3. Select Hit if you want another card.
  4. Select Stay if you do not want another card.
  5. The dealer will continue receiving cards until reaching a score of at least 17.
  6. The hand closest to 21 without going over wins.

Card Values

  • Cards 2 through 10 are worth their displayed value.
  • Jacks, Queens and Kings are worth 10 points.
  • An Ace can be worth 1 or 11 points, depending on the hand.
